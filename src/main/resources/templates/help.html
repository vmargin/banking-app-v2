Local educational simulator

Practice records, not real banking.

Cash - G stores synthetic accounts and practice activity in this application. It is not connected to a bank, payment network, biller, identity provider, or message-delivery service.

How money actions work

  • Cash-in: records a practice credit. It does not draw money from an outside account.
  • Transfers: move practice funds between Cash - G accounts after you review and confirm the recipient and amount.
  • Demo bills: create a simulated ledger entry. No biller receives a payment or settlement notice.
  • Savings goals: move funds between available balance and your own goal. Contributions and withdrawals are not income or spending.
  • Receipts: show the matching local ledger entry. They do not prove that an outside bank or biller was paid.

If a result is unclear

Return to account activity and check for the entry or its receipt before retrying. A page response alone does not confirm an external payment; this simulator makes no external payment.

Account access and support

Change your PIN from Settings by confirming your current four-digit PIN. The current browser signs out after a successful change. Use a synthetic practice PIN and never enter a real banking PIN.

Forgotten-PIN recovery, identity verification, disputes, external support, and complaint channels are not configured in this local project. Do not enter real identity or account information.
